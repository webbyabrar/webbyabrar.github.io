Services

Website Design and SEO Services for Agencies

Pick one service or hand over the full build. Each one is delivered quietly under your agency's name.

01

Website Design and Development

Custom websites designed around your client's business and built to load quickly on every screen size. I work in WordPress with Elementor Pro, GoHighLevel, and hand-coded sites hosted on GitHub, so the platform always fits the project.

02

On-Page SEO

Title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, keyword placement and content layout, set up page by page. Your SEO team starts with a site that already follows best practice instead of fixing it after launch.

03

Internal Linking

A clear link structure that connects service pages, location pages and supporting content. It helps visitors find the next page and helps search engines understand which pages matter most.

04

Image Optimization

Images converted to WebP, resized, compressed and given descriptive file names and alt text. Pages stay sharp, load faster and give Google more context about what each image shows.

05

Schema Markup

JSON-LD structured data for LocalBusiness, Organization, Service, FAQ and more, written for the page it sits on and tested before handover. It gives search engines and AI tools clean facts about the business.

Process

How Our White Label Process Works

  1. Step 1: Share the Project Brief

    Send the details on WhatsApp or through the form: your client's niche, the pages needed, the platform and any design references.

  2. Step 2: Get a Timeline and Quote

    You receive a clear delivery date and price based on the number of pages and features.

  3. Step 3: Design and Development

    The site is designed and built from scratch, with SEO structure added while the pages take shape.

  4. Step 4: Review and Changes

    You review the site with your team or your client, and I make every change you request. Revisions are unlimited until you are satisfied.

  5. Step 5: Handover Under Your Brand

    The finished, optimized website is handed over to you, ready to launch and present as your agency's work.
